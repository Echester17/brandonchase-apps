Quick answers

A few common questions

Do I need to allow notifications?

No. Notifications are optional. Dots still works if you decline them, and you can change permission later in iOS Settings.

Why isn’t my Apple Watch showing the latest week?

Check that the Watch is paired and the Dots companion is installed. Open Dots on your iPhone; it sends an updated snapshot when it becomes active or when a day or setting changes.

Where is my Dots information stored?

Your marked dates and display preference are stored on your iPhone and shared with the Dots widget. The current week and display preference are also sent to your paired Apple Watch. Read the Dots privacy policy.

What should I include in a support email?

Tell me what you expected and what happened, plus your iPhone or Apple Watch model and software version if you know them. Please don’t include passwords or other sensitive information.
