Dots · Privacy

Your days are yours.

Dots is a simple day tracker for iPhone, widgets, and Apple Watch. This policy describes the information used by the app.

Effective October 8, 2026

●

Dots does not send your tracking data to us. Your marked days and display preference are kept on your Apple devices, with a small current-week snapshot shared with your paired Apple Watch.

What Dots stores

Dots stores the dates you mark and your night-mode preference in app storage on your iPhone. The iPhone app and its widget use Apple’s shared app storage so the widget can show the same information.

Apple Watch companion

If Dots is paired with and installed on your Apple Watch, the iPhone sends the current week’s day statuses and your night-mode setting to the Watch app using Apple’s WatchConnectivity. This is a device-to-device feature; Dots does not send that snapshot to a server operated by Brandon Chase Consulting LLC.

Information we do not collect

The Dots app code reviewed for this policy contains no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking service. Its iPhone and widget privacy manifests declare tracking off and no collected data. The reviewed project has no app network requests or third-party analytics SDKs.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Dots schedules a repeating reminder on your device for 8 p.m. local time. It is a local notification, not a message sent from our server. You can manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

Your choices

Notifications are optional. You can change your marked days and display preference in Dots, and control the app’s notification permission through iOS Settings. Apple Watch sharing is used only when the companion is paired and installed.

Changes and questions

If Dots’ data practices change, this policy will be updated. For questions, email coleandrenee@icloud.com.
