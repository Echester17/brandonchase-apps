Dots · Privacy

Your days are yours.

Dots is a simple day tracker for iPhone and widgets. This policy describes the information used by the app.

Effective October 17, 2026

●

Dots does not send your tracking data to us. Your marked days and display preference are kept in app storage on your iPhone. Your Dots widgets use Apple’s shared app storage to show the same information.

What Dots stores

Dots stores the dates you mark and your night-mode preference in app storage on your iPhone. The iPhone app and its widget use Apple’s shared app storage so the widget can show the same information.

Apple Watch

Dots does not currently include an Apple Watch companion and does not sync your information to an Apple Watch.

Information we do not collect

The Dots app code reviewed for this policy contains no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking service. Its iPhone and widget privacy manifests declare tracking off and no collected data. The reviewed project has no app network requests or third-party analytics SDKs.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Dots schedules a repeating reminder on your device for 8 p.m. local time. It is a local notification, not a message sent from our server. You can manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

Your choices

Notifications are optional. You can change your marked days and display preference in Dots, and control the app’s notification permission through iOS Settings. Your widgets show the same information through Apple’s shared app storage.

Changes and questions

If Dots’ data practices change, this policy will be updated. For questions, email coleandrenee@icloud.com.
